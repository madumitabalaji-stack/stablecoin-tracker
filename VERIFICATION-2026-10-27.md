**Verdict key:** ✅ VERIFIED (primary doc confirms cite + substance) · ⚠️ DISCREPANCY (primary doc differs from tracker) · ❓ CAN'T CONFIRM (primary doc unreachable or claim not verifiable from it)

## Summary

All 51 jurisdictions checked against the primary document (codified statute text, enrolled bill, or the administering regulator's own page). Status after follow-up verification of the flagged items:

- **3 real citation errors to fix:** Maine (#14), Nebraska (#44), New Jersey (#17, wrong bill number).
- **1 refine (date is right, add chapter-law cite):** Florida (#04).
- **3 naming fixes:** Iowa (#32), Colorado (#07), Wisconsin (#51).
- **1 enrichment:** Louisiana (#40, add original enactment).
- **Resolved on follow-up (now verified):** New York proposed Part 202 (#01 ✅), New Mexico (#47 ✅ via FID), Montana (#41 ✅ consistent).
- **The rest: verified**, several with a minor "sub-cite/effective-date not separately re-pulled" note.

### Action list (highest value first) — the 3 real errors are all primary-source confirmed
1. **Maine (#14) — WRONG CHAPTER.** Change "32 M.R.S. ch. 80" → **32 M.R.S. ch. 79-A "Money Transmitters," §6067** ("the Maine Money Transmission Modernization Act," PL 2023, c. 662 / LD 2112). §6067 confirmed verbatim. Ch. 80 is the Check Cashers / VC-Kiosks chapter.
2. **Nebraska (#44) — MISLABELED CITE.** §8-3001 (verbatim): "Sections **8-3001 to 8-3031** shall be known and may be cited as the Nebraska Financial Innovation Act" (LB 649, 2021). The tracker's **§8-2724 is NOT the NFIA** (it's "Authorized delegate; procedures" in the Money Transmitters Act). Relabel NFIA to §§8-3001–8-3031; keep §8-2715 as the MT Act VC cite.
3. **New Jersey (#17) — WRONG BILL NUMBER (fixed).** A3454 was the wrong bill; the Digital Asset and Blockchain Technology Act is **A2249 (2024–2025), companion S1304** (pending). Corrected in the data.
4. **Florida (#04) — ADD CHAPTER LAW.** Add **Ch. 2026-176** (and the §560.504 pincite); general effective date June 26, 2026 (Oct. 1, 2026 is correct for the stablecoin provisions, §§560.501–560.504). Confirmed verbatim.
5. **Iowa (#32) — RENAME.** §533C.101 (verbatim): short title is the **"Uniform Money Transmission Modernization Act,"** not "Uniform Money Services Act."
6. **Colorado (#07) / Wisconsin (#51) — "MODEL".** Official short titles are "**Model** Money Transmission Modernization Act" (CO, signed 04/18/2025; Aug. 6 2025 effective date is secondary) and "**Model** Money Transmission Modernization Law" (WI).
7. **Louisiana (#40) — ADD ORIGINAL ENACTMENT.** VCBA originally enacted by **Act 341 (2020 R.S.)**, amended by SB 185 / Act 331 (2023); confirm §§6:1381–6:1394 against the codified statute.
8. **Spot-checks before pinning quotes/dates:** Illinois quoted "stable nominal value" definition (#06), Oklahoma Nov. 1 2026 date (#45), Mississippi July 1 2026 date (#48); optionally add South Carolina's **Act 208 / R131** pincite (#21).
